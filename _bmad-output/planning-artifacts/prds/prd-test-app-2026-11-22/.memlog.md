- (decision) PRD covers the whole project; already-shipped MVP features documented as existing requirements, new work (liabilities, budgets, analytics, daily limit) as new FRs
- (decision) Coaching path chosen over fast path; PRD written for the author's own use, not for investors or customers
- (event) workspace bound, prd.md skeleton created; source brief (final) at briefs/brief-test-app-2026-11-22
- (decision) Vision covers both spending and saving: a savings norm is added to scope and savings funds are reserved alongside mandatory outflows
- (decision) Monetization is not planned for now; PRD keeps it out of scope as background only
- (decision) Positioning wording in the vision: 'лучшие практики в учёте финансов' instead of 'корпоративный учёт для частного лица'
- (decision) Savings norm = user-set percentage taken from every income, transferred immediately after the income arrives
- (decision) Savings are a separate group of accounts; settings hold the target savings account; the automatic deduction is recorded as a transfer between accounts (not expense, not in P&L)
- (decision) Income form gets a checkbox 'не списывать % в накопления' to skip the savings transfer for that income
- (decision) Savings reserve is always held: under shortfall the daily limit drops to zero, savings are not raided automatically
- (decision) Daily limit base = spending accounts only; savings accounts are excluded from the limit entirely (supersedes the earlier 'single target account in settings' idea)
- (decision) Savings transfer is linked to the income operation and is deleted together with it
- (decision) Savings percentage is a per-account setting asked for when a savings account is created; several savings accounts are allowed, each with its own percentage
- (decision) Sub-kopeck amounts are rounded down; if no savings account exists the norm is a no-op
- (decision) Income splits across all savings accounts at once; total of percentages across savings accounts may not exceed 100% and creating an account that would exceed it is blocked
- (decision) Editing an income deletes the old linked savings transfer and creates a new one
- (decision) Rounding: sub-kopeck remainder is truncated, accepted as negligible loss (5.446 -> 5.44)
- (decision) Main screen shows three figures in order: spending accounts total, savings total, all accounts total including savings (supersedes current totalBalance-only behavior)
- (assumption) UJ-4 (mortgage payment split) is a designed scenario: author has no mortgage today, the journey is written to define the obligation flow, not from an observed session
- (decision) Obligation payment flow: occurrence in the payment calendar, user marks it paid, amount is debited from the account chosen on the obligation, remaining debt decreases
- (decision) Mandatory outflows = payment calendar occurrences only (planned and recurring expenses plus obligation payments); transfers and budgets are excluded
- (decision) No planned income in the calendar means no daily limit is computed; the app offers to add income to the calendar instead
- (decision) Forward deficit check looks one month ahead from today and subtracts it from free money
- (decision) Overdue unpaid payments count in the reserve first; negative free money shows 0 per day plus the shortfall amount and the next income date
- (decision) Obligation recurrence: monthly payment until the debt reaches zero; then the series stops and the obligation moves to archive
- (decision) Manual correction of remaining principal is not allowed after creation (supersedes the brief): the amount must be verified at creation, later only payments change it
- (decision) If the payment day already passed when the obligation is created, the app asks whether the payment was made and offers to record it
- (decision) Early repayment and floating rate stay out of scope v1 (open questions)
- (decision) Budgets exist for all expense categories; 'хотелка' and 'нежелательная' are analytics markers, unwanted is a subset of wants shown with a red marker
- (decision) No budget rollover: each month starts from zero
- (decision) Creating a budget above free money is blocked; if free money later drops, budgets shrink proportionally with a notification, original value shown struck through, and restored when money returns
- (decision) Analytics: wants/unwanted only as markers in category lists and reports, no separate block; the period switch exists on the overview and in reports
- (decision) Final obligation payment is capped at remaining debt plus interest; P&L takes interest only; balance must reconcile
- (decision) A payment smaller than monthly interest triggers a debt-growth warning at obligation creation; late payments are dated today and clear the overdue state
