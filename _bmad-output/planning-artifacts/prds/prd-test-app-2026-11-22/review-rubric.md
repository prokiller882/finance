# PRD Quality Review — Финансы (test-app, 2026-11-22)

**Basis.** Read in full: `prd.md` (draft, 660 lines), `brief.md` (final), the brief's `addendum.md` (outside this run folder; there is no `addendum.md` here), and the rubric. Calibration: solo product, single user, no backend, no investors, no team. Missing personas, stakeholder sign-off, SLAs, compliance and enterprise NFRs are not penalized. The PRD *is* the build source for a three-month phase and feeds story creation, so done-ness and internal consistency are judged at full strength.

## Overall verdict

The spine is real — one derived number, three accounting contours, obligations as an outflow inside the same limit — and the shipped app's date, kopeck and archive rules are carried with unusual precision. The money-movement requirements do not yet agree with each other: FR-17/FR-18, FR-13, FR-22/FR-25 and FR-33/FR-34 describe four different products, and §1 claims the differentiation the brief and its research explicitly retract. It is a good description of what exists and a risky source for building obligations, budgets and the savings norm.

## Decision-readiness — thin

The PRD states decisions as decisions — FR-20 is blunt about forbidding manual correction, FR-34 chooses automatic cuts over warning, §6 closes nine boundaries — so this is not a smoothed document. What it does not do is price the choices. The largest one is invisible: the savings norm removes a slice of every income from the number the product exists to display, and the only argument is one Vision sentence. The second is FR-20, where a stated principle of the brief is reversed and the reason is nowhere. A reader pushing back on either would find the objection neither acknowledged nor dodged; it simply is not there.

### Findings

- **[critical]** The savings norm is folded into the headline question and the limit's arithmetic without a trade-off (§1; §4.5 FR-22–FR-26; §7.1) — the brief's question ends at "не пропустить обязательный платёж"; §1 adds "**и всё-таки отложить**" and asserts "норма сбережений резервируется первыми". FR-27's formula contains no norm term; the money stays out of the limit only because FR-23 transfers it first. Nobody states what is given up: every percent of norm lowers the daily number, and it now competes with the brief's "обязательства первыми". *Fix:* add a short trade-off paragraph (norm vs limit vs obligations, with precedence and reason), and either put the norm into FR-27 or say explicitly that it acts solely through FR-23's prior transfer.
- **[high]** FR-20 reverses the brief's correction principle without naming the reversal or giving a reason (§4.4 FR-20; §9.3; §10) — the brief says "**Остаток можно править руками в любой момент — цифра банка важнее расчётной**" and lists "ручная коррекция" in the first version. The PRD says "Остаток меняется исключительно оплатой вхождений" and offers only `[ASSUMPTION: способ исправления расхождения не выбран]`. §9.3 reads as an internal design question, not as a reversal of a stated principle, so the reader cannot weigh it. *Fix:* say "это отменяет принцип брифа… потому что…" in FR-20, and record the consequence — until Q3 is decided the product has **no** correction path, which is a regression against the brief.
- **[high]** FR-34 auto-cuts user-set ceilings with no argument for overriding them (§4.7 FR-34; §10) — "приложение пропорционально урезает бюджеты" means the app silently rewrites a number the user chose; §10 only notes "правило приоритетов не задано". The real question — cut, warn, or freeze allocation — is never asked. *Fix:* decide and defend the behaviour, including whether a manual edit after a cut is sticky.
- **[medium]** Open Questions are ordered by topic, not by what blocks the build (§9) — deposit accounting (Q2) and early repayment (Q4) are already non-goals in §6/§7.3, yet remain "open"; Q3, the only one that blocks FR-20 and FR-18, gets one paragraph; Q5 is cosmetic. *Fix:* split into "blocks implementation" (Q3, Q1 accuracy, Q6) and "later", and close or reframe the questions that §6 already answers.
- **[medium]** The daily-limit horizon is bounded to one month with no reason (§4.6 FR-27; §10) — §10 says "«месяц от сегодня» трактуется как та же дата следующего календарного месяца", but the brief's rule 3 was unbounded ("Если после него обязательных списаний больше, чем поступлений, разница вычитается из лимита заранее"). The bound makes the limit more optimistic than the brief's rule, and the trade-off (precision vs. complexity) is not argued. *Fix:* one sentence on why one month, and what happens on the 31st when the anniversary month is shorter.

## Substance over theater — adequate

No personas, no boilerplate: the NFRs are product-specific (50 ms, 1 s, 16 px, 44 px, WCAG AA, 360/1024 px), and the "three contours" framing does real work — FR-18's separation of P&L / ДДС / баланс is the most valuable paragraph in the document. The theater is concentrated in one place: the Vision's differentiation sentence, which is exactly the claim the brief and its research retract. The 50-FR set also contains two verbatim duplicate pairs, which inflates apparent coverage.

### Findings

- **[critical]** §1 claims a differentiation the brief explicitly denies (§1; brief §«Чем это отличается»; addendum) — §1: "**Отличие от трекеров расходов не в отчётности, а в производной цифре**". Brief: "**Пока ничем, кроме точности под одного человека**" and "Дневной лимит — индустриальный стандарт… формула везде одна". Addendum: "**Сама механика — не отличие**". This is innovation theater in the one place the PRD could least afford it. *Fix:* either soften §1 to the brief's position ("механика — стандарт; отличие пока не найдено") or keep the claim and record it as an explicit disagreement with the brief in §9.
- **[medium]** FR-3/FR-42 and FR-31/FR-43 are duplicate requirements (§4.1 vs §4.9; §4.6 vs §4.9) — FR-3 and FR-42 both specify the three overview sums; FR-31 and FR-43 both specify the limit card. They will diverge the first time one is edited. *Fix:* keep one home and cross-reference the other, or merge.
- **[medium]** The reliability NFR promises a backup the product never creates (§5 "Надёжность"; FR-47) — "при повреждённом хранилище приложение не теряет копию молча" presumes a copy; FR-47 only offers manual export/import and never defines a backup artifact. *Fix:* either define the copy (when it is made, where it lives, how the user sees its date) or narrow the NFR to "не перезаписывает данные при ошибке импорта".

## Strategic coherence — adequate

There is a thesis — one number, ten seconds, one hand, offline, before the purchase — and the daily limit, the calendar and the overview follow from it. But the PRD deleted the problem that gives the thesis its force. Without the brief's "средства закончились **за 7 дней до аванса**" incident and the credit-failure loop, §7.1's obligations-first ordering has no cause, SM-1/SM-2 read as generic prudence, and the savings norm arrives as a plausible nice-to-have rather than a bet. The metric set also confuses outcome with activity.

### Findings

- **[high]** The problem section is gone (§1 → §2; brief §«Проблема») — the brief's origin incident and its diagnosis ("Обязательство живёт отдельно от повседневных трат, и именно на этом стыке ломается планирование") appear nowhere; UJ-3 dramatizes the squeeze but not its cause. This is why §7 looks arbitrary and why the PRD cannot justify its own priority order. *Fix:* restore a 5–8 line "Проблема" between §1 and §2, and point §7.1 and SM-1/SM-2 at it.
- **[medium]** §7.1's ordering has no rationale (§7.1) — the brief's one-line argument, "именно на кредитах люди ошибаются чаще всего", was dropped, so obligations-first is inherited rather than defended, and the newly added savings bullet sits beside it with equal weight. *Fix:* restore the rationale at the head of §7.1 and order the bullets under it.
- **[medium]** Success metrics mix uninstrumentable outcomes with activity (§8) — SM-1 ("не берёт ни одного займа до зарплаты") has no data source: the product has no concept of a loan and no telemetry, yet claims "Проверяет FR-27, FR-28, FR-31", which measure none of it. SM-3 ("не реже пяти дней в неделю") is pure activity and unmeasurable by design (local data, no analytics). SM-C1's "разумного минимума" has no threshold. *Fix:* mark SM-1 as self-reported, replace SM-3 with an outcome (e.g. limit checked before a purchase), and give SM-C1 a number or drop it.

## Done-ness clarity — thin

Most FRs do carry a verifiable consequence, and the date edge cases (FR-11), the worked limit example (FR-27) and the kopeck rule (FR-6) are genuinely testable. But the four clusters that carry the new money movement either contradict each other or assert invariants the product cannot display. This is the dimension downstream story creation leans on hardest, and §4.4 and §4.7 are not safe to lean on yet.

### Findings

- **[critical]** FR-17 creates a full-amount expense while FR-18/FR-37 put only interest in P&L (§4.4 FR-17, FR-18; §4.8 FR-37; §4.2 FR-6) — FR-17: "Со счёта списания создаётся **операция-расход на сумму платежа**". FR-18: "**В P&L попадают только проценты**; в ДДС — весь платёж". A plain expense operation flows into P&L at full amount (FR-37), so the two requirements cannot both hold; no split or obligation-specific operation type is defined, and the glossary's "Операция" has only расход/доход/перевод. FR-6 also requires a category for expenses, and FR-15 gives the obligation none. *Fix:* define the payment as a split posting (principal + interest, or an expense plus a liability movement) and name the category rule.
- **[critical]** Deleting a paid operation returns the occurrence to pending but never restores principal, and FR-20 forbids fixing it (§4.2 FR-8; §4.4 FR-20; §4.8 FR-18/FR-39) — FR-8: "Удаление оплаченной операции возвращает связанное вхождение в состояние «ожидает»"; FR-20: "Остаток меняется **исключительно** оплатой вхождений". After deletion the occurrence is unpaid, the debt is still reduced, and FR-18's "баланс сходится" and FR-39's liabilities are permanently wrong with no correction path. The same hole applies to editing an obligation operation's amount, type, account or date. *Fix:* state that unpaying/deleting reverses the principal and re-derives the split, or explicitly forbid deleting obligation operations.
- **[high]** FR-13's forecast has no sign, no account scope and a different horizon from the limit (§4.3 FR-13; §4.6 FR-27) — "Прогноз = текущий баланс + неоплаченные вхождения до конца месяца, включая просрочку" does not say whether expenses enter as negatives, whether "текущий баланс" is all accounts or expense accounts only (savings are in §4.1 totals), or why the horizon is the calendar month while free money uses "месяц от сегодня". As written, adding an expense could raise the forecast. *Fix:* write the formula with signed terms and named accounts, and state the horizon once for both.
- **[high]** FR-33 and FR-34 make a monthly ceiling that shrinks with every purchase (§4.7) — budgets must be ≤ free money at save (FR-33); free money falls with every expense; therefore "если свободные деньги уменьшились ниже суммы бюджетов, приложение пропорционально урезает бюджеты" fires on the first coffee of the day and keeps firing, and "бюджеты восстанавливаются до исходных значений" can only happen at the next income. "Сумма урезанных равна свободным деньгам" also has no rounding rule in integer kopecks, so exact equality is not generally achievable. *Fix:* trigger the cut only on reserve changes (calendar/obligations), not on discretionary spending; define rounding and what "исходный размер" means after a manual edit.
- **[high]** "Only future incomes" is contradicted by the edit path (§4.5 FR-22 vs FR-25) — FR-22: "Изменение процента влияет только на будущие приходы"; FR-25: "Изменение суммы, счёта или даты прихода удаляет старые переводы и создаёт новые". Editing an old income after a percent change recreates its transfers at the current percent, retroactively rewriting savings. Nothing stores the applied percent on the transfer. *Fix:* snapshot the percent (or amount) on the transfer and recreate from the snapshot; state whether FR-24's opt-out survives an edit.
- **[high]** FR-35's testable consequence is a disjunction the PRD says is undecided (§4.7 FR-35) — "Экран доступен из настроек **или** обзора" is listed under "Проверяемые следствия" while the same FR carries `[ASSUMPTION: точка входа … не выбрана]`. A test cannot pass both ways. *Fix:* move the entry point out of the consequences until Q6 is decided.
- **[high]** FR-18's balance invariant references an entity the product does not report (§4.4 FR-18; §4.8 FR-39) — "изменение активов равно изменению обязательств плюс изменение **капитала**". "Капитал" is not in the glossary and FR-39's balance has only assets and liabilities, so the invariant is unobservable. *Fix:* either add equity to the balance report or restate the check as "Δактивы = −тело + (−проценты)" tied to visible lines.
- **[medium]** FR-4 writes off a balance without saying where it goes, and ignores obligations (§4.1 FR-4; §4.4) — "остаток списывается" does not say whether a write-off is an expense, an equity adjustment, or invisible; it cannot be verified in P&L/ДДС. The deletion guard lists "операциями, планами или переводами" but not obligations, which hold `accountId` for debiting. *Fix:* name the posting and add obligations to the guard.
- **[medium]** FR-12's "Оплатить" is undefined for income occurrences and the occurrence state machine is never named (§4.3 FR-12; §4.2 FR-8) — "«Оплатить» создаёт операцию с сегодняшней датой" does not say what type is created when the occurrence is an income, nor how that interacts with FR-23's norm. States drift between "ожидает" (FR-8), "оплаченные" (FR-12) and "просрочка" (FR-14) with no glossary entry. *Fix:* define pending/paid/skipped and the action names per occurrence type.
- **[medium]** FR-7 never says which filters exist, and FR-14's cap is ambiguous (§4.2 FR-7; §4.3 FR-14) — "с фильтрами" is not testable; "затем платежи на 30 дней вперёд, не более трёх" could mean three total or three upcoming after the overdue ones. *Fix:* enumerate filters (type, account, category, amount?) and disambiguate the cap.
- **[medium]** FR-15's late-payment flow has no outcome, and field validation is absent (§4.4 FR-15) — "приложение спрашивает, был ли платёж, и предлагает его провести" does not say what happens on yes/no or which month's occurrence is created; there is no rule for rate range, day 1–31, payment > 0, or remainder ≤ loan amount. *Fix:* specify the branch outcomes and the validation table.
- **[medium]** FR-47 asserts a schema that cannot contain obligations (§4.10 FR-47) — "Копия схемы v3 восстанавливает планы, вхождения и **обязательства**; копия v2 мигрирует без потерь". Obligations are new in this PRD; the current schema is v3. Either v3 is being redefined or obligations require v4 and a migration, and the PRD says nothing about the migration, the export version field, or corrupt-file handling. *Fix:* name the target schema version and the migration in the obligations FR cluster.
- **[low]** Remaining consequences are adjectives or arithmetic that cannot land exactly (§4.7 FR-40; §4.10 FR-48; §4.6 FR-31 vs FR-30) — "Сумма долей равна 100%" with rounded percentages; "честно различает «защищено» и «может быть очищено»" with no defined strings; FR-31 shows "остаток свободных денег" while FR-30 says "отрицательное число в карточке лимита не выводится" — which of the two is hidden? *Fix:* add a 0.1 pp tolerance, name the storage states, and say which line goes blank.
- **[low]** Obligation occurrences do not inherit FR-11's date rules (§4.3 FR-11 vs §4.4 FR-16) — FR-11 says "31-е число в коротком месяце переносится на последний день месяца", FR-16 creates a monthly occurrence from "день платежа" with no reference to that rule, so a 31st payment day is unspecified. *Fix:* state that FR-11's shift applies, and cap the series horizon.

## Scope honesty — thin

§6 is real, the assumptions index exists, and UJ-4 is honestly labelled projective — better than most solo PRDs. But §7 is not the scope of *this* document; it is the scope of the next phase, written as if the shipped product did not exist. And the biggest addition since the brief — savings — is presented as original scope.

### Findings

- **[high]** Savings accounts and the norm enter both the Vision and the three-month MVP with no departure note (§1; §4.5; §7.1; §10) — the brief has no savings accounts, no norm, no automatic transfers; the nearest statement is a 2–3 year "сколько можно отложить". §10 flags only the *storage location* of the percent ("решение принято в ходе работы над PRD"), which makes the addition look smaller than it is: it changes the limit base, the overview (FR-3), the operation model (FR-23/25) and SM-4. *Fix:* add a §10 entry and a §7.1 note saying savings are new since the brief, why they precede obligations, and that FR-3 follows from them — or move the norm to §7.2.
- **[high]** §7 covers the next phase, not the whole product it claims to scope (§7; §0) — `Scope MVP` lists five bullets, all new work; shipped features (accounts, operations, journal, calendar, P&L/ДДС/balance, settings/theme/import) appear in no phase at all, and FR-41's category marks are main-section requirements but deferred in §7.2. A reader cannot tell built from planned, which matters most for the obligations schema work. *Fix:* add a "выпущено" column or subsection, and mark each FR's phase.
- **[high]** The brief's "оценка" affordance is silently dropped (brief §«Баланс: обязательства»; §4.4) — the brief promises "Всё расчётное идёт с пометкой «оценка»"; the PRD keeps the accuracy caveat only as open question 1 and never marks computed interest or principal in the UI. Given FR-18 rounds interest down, the label is the user's only signal that the debt figure is approximate. *Fix:* add the label as a consequence of FR-18/FR-19 or record it as a deliberate drop.
- **[medium]** §0 and §7.1 disagree about what the three months are (§0; §7.1) — §0: "ближайший трёхмесячный этап (**обязательства**) и следующий шаг (бюджеты и аналитика)"; §7.1 puts the daily limit, savings and the overview in the same three months. One of the two is a stale shorthand. *Fix:* rewrite §0's parenthetical to match §7.1.
- **[medium]** The same items are permanently out in §6 and pending in §7.3/§9 (§6; §7.3; §9.4) — "Досрочное погашение и плавающая ставка" is a non-goal in §6, "отложены (открытый вопрос 4)" in §7.3, and still open in §9. "Вклады" is likewise a non-goal and an open question. *Fix:* one status per item: out, or open, not both.
- **[medium]** §0 attributes the competitor landscape to the brief, which does not contain it (§0; brief; addendum) — "ландшафт конкурентов и коммерческая гипотеза остаются в брифе". The landscape, sources and the "источник вендорский, не нейтральный" caveat live in `addendum.md`. Dropping the pointer also drops the caveats. *Fix:* cite `brief.md` and `addendum.md`, and note that the addendum's "CFO для себя / фрилансеры" segment is deliberately not adopted.
- **[medium]** FR-45 removes a shipped block without saying it is a change (§4.9 FR-45; brief; current app) — "«Ближайшие платежи» на обзоре отсутствуют" contradicts the brief's softer "переезжают в календарь" and the current UI. For a brownfield PRD, a removed behaviour must be marked as removed, not stated as a timeless fact. *Fix:* "блок перенесён в календарь; на обзоре его нет" and note it as a change from the shipped version.
- **[low]** Installation is assumed but never required (§2.3 UJ-1; §4.10 FR-48) — UJ-1 opens with "приложение уже установлено", the brief says it "стоит на телефоне", but FR-48 covers only caching and storage status. For a one-person product this is a one-time act, so low. *Fix:* one line that install is out of scope or covered by the PWA manifest.

## Downstream usability — adequate

Glossary, contiguous IDs and section-level pull-out-ability are present — more than the rubric usually gets. The blocker is one word with two meanings, plus a glossary definition of free money that does not match the formula it is supposed to define.

### Findings

- **[high]** "Обязательство" means both a loan entity and a balance-sheet aggregate (§3; §4.4; §4.8 FR-39) — the glossary defines it as "кредит … с остатком основного долга, ставкой, платежом из договора, днём платежа и счётом списания"; FR-39 uses the same word for "сумма остатков основного долга активных обязательств". Downstream will conflate the entity with the report line. *Fix:* rename the report line ("кредитная задолженность" / "долг по кредитам") and add it to the glossary.
- **[medium]** The glossary's "Свободные деньги" omits the term FR-27 actually uses (§3 vs §4.6 FR-27) — glossary: "минус дефицит обязательных списаний после него в пределах месяца от сегодня"; FR-27 subtracts `max(0, списания − плановые поступления)` and counts outflows "по дату ближайшего поступления **включительно**". The glossary has neither the receipts offset nor the inclusive boundary, so the two definitions give different numbers. *Fix:* make the glossary restate FR-27 verbatim, or point it at FR-27.
- **[medium]** Terms used in FRs but absent from the glossary (§3 vs §4) — "капитал" (FR-18), "плановое поступление" (FR-27), "серия" (FR-11/12/16), "хотелка"/"нежелательная" (FR-32/41), "прогноз" (FR-13), occurrence status "ожидает"/"оплаченные" (FR-8/12). *Fix:* add them or replace with defined terms.
- **[low]** Three UJs have no named protagonist and references point backwards (§2.3; §4.9 FR-42) — only UJ-1 names "stas" (lowercase); UJ-2/3/4 are subjectless, and FR-42's "Порядок фиксирован" is meaningless without FR-3's stated order. *Fix:* name the protagonist once and reuse; restate FR-42's order inline.

## Shape fit — adequate

Solo, single-operator, brownfield: the right shape is a capability spec with one or two load-bearing UJs. This is close. UJ-1's "ten seconds, one hand, in a shop" is the product's acceptance test and earns its place; UJ-4 is projective and says so. The mismatch is structural: §0 promises a whole-product document, §7 delivers a phase plan, and §4 mixes shipped and unbuilt capabilities without a marker.

### Findings

- **[medium]** The document is two shapes at once (whole product vs phase plan) (§0; §4; §7) — §0: "описывает продукт целиком: выпущенный MVP, ближайший трёхмесячный этап…"; §7: a five-bullet plan for the next three months. UJs are the right weight for this product, but they are attached to a scope section that does not include half the FRs. *Fix:* choose one shape — a capability spec with phase tags per FR, or a phase PRD that says so in §0 and moves shipped behaviour to an appendix.
